Core Program Components

The core program components focus on maintaining a clean, safe, and healthy school environment for all learners. These components promote proper hygiene and sanitation practices, prevent the spread of diseases, and ensure access to essential health and sanitation facilities. 
Safe Drinking Water

Daily access to clean and safe drinking water within the school premises.

Toilet & Facilities

Adequate, clean, functional, gender-segregated, and private school toilets with proper drainage.

Group Hygiene

Supervised daily group handwashing with soap and toothbrushing with fluoride toothpaste for all learners.

Menstrual Hygiene Management (MHM)

Safe, private spaces for female students, clean water access, and proper disposal mechanisms for sanitary pads.

Environmental Hygiene

Proper solid waste segregation, materials recovery facilities (MRF), and functional septic systems (prohibiting waste burning).

Deworming

Semi-annual deworming programs for schoolchildren.

Food Safety

Safe practices in food handling and preparation, plus sanitary permits for school canteens.

Health Education

Hygiene and sanitation lessons integrated into school routines, supported by stakeholder and Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) involvement.
