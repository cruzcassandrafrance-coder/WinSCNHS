WASH in Schools (WinS) Program

The Department of Education (DepEd) WASH in Schools (WinS) Program is established under DepEd Order No. 10, s. 2016 to promote safe water, sanitation, and hygiene standards in all Philippine public schools.

Core Program Components

The core program components focus on maintaining a clean, safe, and healthy school environment for all learners.

Three Star Approach
DepEd uses a Three-Star Rating System to measure and track school progress toward national WinS standards:

• 1-Star to 3-Star Scale: Evaluates compliance across crucial indicators like pupil-to-toilet seat ratios, daily handwashing practices, and waste management.
• Monitoring: Schools regularly log progress through the official WinS Monitoring Dashboards across regional and division levels.
