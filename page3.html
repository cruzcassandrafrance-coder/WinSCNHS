Facilities

The WinS facilities of Calumpit National High School support the implementation of proper water, sanitation, and hygiene practices within the school. These facilities include toilets, handwashing stations, water supply systems, drainage, and waste management facilities. Their availability, cleanliness, functionality, and regular maintenance help provide a safe, clean, and healthy environment for students, teachers, and other school personnel.
